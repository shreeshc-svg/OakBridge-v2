# Oakbridge Reader / Studio — QA findings, 30 July 2026

Platform: `ebooks.oakbridge.in` (Reader storefront + Studio admin).
**Not** `www.oakbridge.in`, which is a separate codebase and unaffected by
everything below.

Tester: Rohan Kapoor. Triage and severity added afterwards.

**This platform is publicly reachable and linked from the main site.** The
"Interested in e-books? Read on Oakbridge eReader" call to action on the
homepage, the bookstore and every product page sends customers here. Anything
broken below is broken in front of real visitors arriving from www.oakbridge.in.

## P0 — money, tax, or lost customers

### 0. A purchased book may not open — verify first, before anything else
`ebooks.oakbridge.in/reader/2d6eb65f-6d06-4028-a2b1-035b9e6a597b` sits on a
spinner reading **"Loading Manuscript…"**.

The screenshot may simply have been taken mid-load, so this needs confirming
rather than assuming — but if a paid-for book does not open, nothing else on
this list matters. That is the entire product. Check it against the purchased
title ("Binder_Technology and Analytics for Law and Justice", 516 pages) and
time how long it takes; large manuscripts may be loading in full rather than
streaming by chapter.

**Done looks like:** first page renders in a couple of seconds regardless of
manuscript size, with a visible failure state instead of an indefinite spinner.

### 1. Checkout captures no address, and the invoice defaults place of supply
Reported: *"While purchasing a book we need to capture the address as well during
checkout flow."*

Worse than a missing field. The generated invoice (`OAK-2026-27-N/A`) shows:

- Buyer and Consignee: **"Digital Delivery / Online Shelf"** — no customer address
- **Place of Supply: Haryana** — which is the *seller's* state, not the buyer's
- No customer GSTIN field

Under GST, the place of supply for a B2C supply of digital goods is the
recipient's location. With no address captured, every invoice defaults to
Haryana and charges CGST/SGST, when a buyer in Maharashtra should be IGST.
That is a filing exposure on every sale to another state, not a cosmetic gap.

Also note the invoice number contains the literal string **N/A**, and
"Payment Ref" reads **N/A** on a prepaid Razorpay order — the payment reference
should be captured for reconciliation and refunds.

**Done looks like:** billing state captured at checkout (minimum), place of
supply derived from it, correct GST head applied, optional GSTIN field for
business buyers, real invoice number, Razorpay payment ID stored on the invoice.

### 2. Contact form does not deliver
Reported: *"message sent where is it being delivered? I checked
info@oakbridge.in, no message received."*

The form returns a "Thank You! … we'll get back to you within 24 hours"
confirmation, so the customer believes they have been heard. Every enquiry since
launch may have been lost silently.

**Done looks like:** submissions delivered to a monitored mailbox AND persisted
in Studio so nothing depends on email alone; the confirmation screen shown only
after a confirmed write.

### 3. No price or Add to Cart on bookstore tiles
Reported twice. Tiles show title, author, language and page count — no price, no
buy action. A cart and checkout clearly exist (cart shows ₹376), so the storefront
is hiding the one thing a shop is for.

### 4. "Add to Library" appears on paid titles under Trending
Reported: *"Under trending why am I getting option to add them to my library
directly?"*

If that grants access without payment it is a revenue leak; if it only applies to
titles tagged FREE, the button needs to be visibly conditional. Either way the
price → cart → checkout path is missing from the tile.

**Needs a decision:** what "Add to Library" means for a paid title.

## P1 — visibly broken

### 5. Product image does not render in the cart
Confirmed in the screenshot: the cart shows the alt text "The Law" against a
blank box, so the `src` is wrong or the asset is missing. (Alt text doing its job,
at least.)

### 6. Logo does not return to the dashboard
Clicking the Oakbridge logo goes nowhere. Universal convention; users will try it.

### 7. "See all" links go nowhere
On Trending Now and New Releases. The section headers promise a listing page that
does not exist.

### 8. "Save for later" has no visible destination
Reported: *"after clicking Save for later, where is the book getting saved?"*
No confirmation, no list, no way back to it.

### 8b. Product page shows no price, and the back link lies
On `/book/70ff677c-…` the page shows pages, chapters, ISBN, "Lifetime Access"
and a single **Add to Library** button. No price anywhere — not even for titles
that are not tagged FREE. This is the same defect as #3, but on the page where a
buying decision actually gets made.

The back control reads **"Back to library"** even when the visitor arrived from
the Bookstore, which is where every link on the storefront sends them. It should
reflect where they came from, or simply say "Back to bookstore".

Two smaller things on the same page: the description is the filename repeated
(`PRI_Withholding_Tax_Charter`), and the ISBN reads `53424`, which is not a
valid ISBN — see item 11.

**Worth confirming:** "Lifetime Access" is a licensing commitment. Make sure it
matches what the terms of service actually promise.

### 9. Slug not editable in admin — books and users
Reported: *"UR Slug not changing in admin, pls check for users as well."*
Editing appears to save but the slug does not change.

## Working well — worth keeping

Not everything here is broken, and it is worth saying which decisions are good
so they do not get "fixed" later:

- **The search placeholder changes with context** — "Search bookstore" on the
  storefront, "Search your library" on the library page. Small, and exactly
  right.
- **The library page itself is sound**: a "Purchased" shelf with a View All, and
  a "Recommended For You" row beneath it. The problem is only how you reach it.
- **FREE badges on tiles** are clear and legible.
- **The hero banner artwork** ("Your Library. Anytime. Anywhere.") is good — the
  complaint in item 14 is that the container crops it, not the image itself.

## Cross-product note

Items 13, 14 and 17 have already been solved on `www.oakbridge.in` — breadcrumbs,
a fit/fill choice per banner, and a 24-per-page infinite scroll. If the two
products are meant to feel like one company, the Reader team could take those
decisions rather than re-litigate them.
